Buontalenti®:
The Gelato That Made History

Florentine tradition says gelato was born at the Medici court in the 16th century, thanks to the architect Bernardo Buontalenti. Our signature flavour carries his name.

1500s
The Origin

As tradition has it, Bernardo Buontalenti invents gelato in Renaissance Florence, for the Medici court.

1932
The House

Idilio Badiani opens his gelateria in Florence. Later, Paolo Pomposi makes Buontalenti® the house signature: 4 simple ingredients (cream, milk, sugar, eggs).

TODAY

Il Barattolo GELATO

Thanks to the success of our Buontalenti®, Buontalenti® Pistacchio, and La Dolcevita flavours, we have developed Il Barattolo GELATO — a premium line designed for large-scale distribution.

This innovative format brings the authentic quality of our gelato to a wider audience, making it more accessible without compromising on ingredient excellence or the Florentine tradition.

With Il Barattolo Gelato, everyone can enjoy the rich, creamy flavours that have made Badiani famous, now in a convenient 500ml format.
